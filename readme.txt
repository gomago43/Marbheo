Survive endless waves of zombies in this retro-style action shooter!

Face off against increasingly fast and deadly undead hordes. Use your aim and reflexes to hold your ground as long as you can. How many waves can you survive before you're overrun?

Tileset: Zombie Apocalypse Tileset by Ittai Manero – https://ittaimanero.itch.io/zombie-apocalypse-tileset
